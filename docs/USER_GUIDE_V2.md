4. Copy the key (starts with `AIzaSy...`)
5. Paste it in MeshCards
6. ✅ Keep generating unlimited decks!

**Your key is NEVER stored** - it's only used for that one generation.

## ❓ FAQ

### Q: Is my API key stored?
**A:** No! It's only used for that one generation, then discarded.

### Q: How many decks can I make with my own key?
**A:** Unlimited! Google gives 1500 free requests/day for Gemini.

### Q: Why are premium models sponsors-only?
**A:** Novita costs money per API call. The $5/month helps cover those costs while keeping basic features free for everyone.

### Q: Can I use my own Novita key?
**A:** Not yet, but we might add this in the future!

### Q: I sponsored but don't see premium models
**A:** Takes up to 24 hours for auto-sync. Need it faster? Email with your BuyMeACoffee receipt.

### Q: What if I cancel my sponsorship?
**A:** You'll lose access to Novita models but keep all other features. Your past decks are yours forever!

## 🚀 Tips & Tricks

### Getting the Most Out of Free Tier

1. **Use BYOK**: Get unlimited generations with your free Google key
2. **Be specific**: Better prompts = better cards = fewer regenerations
3. **Batch process**: Upload multiple PDFs at once (max 3)
4. **Off-peak hours**: Less likely to hit rate limits at night

### Getting the Most Out of Premium

1. **Long PDFs**: Use Qwen 2.5 72B for textbooks (128K context!)
2. **Complex topics**: Use Llama 405B for best quality
3. **Speed**: Use Llama 70B for fast, high-quality generations
4. **Multilingual**: Use Mistral Large 2 for non-English content

### When to Use Which Model

```
📚 Long textbook     → Qwen 2.5 72B (128K context)
🧠 Complex concepts  → Llama 3.1 405B (smartest)
⚡ Quick generation  → Gemini 2.5 Flash (fastest)
🌍 非英語コンテンツ    → Mistral Large 2 (multilingual)
💰 Save money        → Gemini (free!)
```
