# 🎯 Quick Start User Guide

Welcome to MeshCards! This guide will help you transform your notes into study materials efficiently.

## 🏁 Getting Started (3 Steps)

1. **Upload or Paste**: Drag and drop your PDFs/Notes or paste raw text into the input field.
2. **Configure**: Select your preferred AI model and deck name in the right sidebar.
3. **Generate**: Click "Generate Flashcards" and wait for the magic to happen.

---

## 🔑 Handling API Limits (BYOK)

If you see a **"Daily Limit Reached"** or **"API Key Required"** message:

1. **Get a Free Key**: Go to [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey) (30 seconds).
2. **Enter Key**: Click the Settings icon (or the status indicator in the configuration panel).
3. **Save**: Paste your key under the appropriate provider (Gemini or Novita).
4. **Result**: Generate unlimited decks using your own API quota!

---

## 🧠 Choosing the Right Model

We offer two classes of intelligence engines:

- **Standard (Core Gemini)**:
  - Best for: General topics, long files, and super-fast generation.
  - Recommended: **Gemini 1.5 Flash**.
- **Rare Models (High Reasoning)**:
  - Best for: Complex STEM subjects, textbooks, and high-accuracy requirements.
  - Recommended: **Llama 3.3 70B** (Sponsor only or BYOK).

---

## 🎨 Best Practices for Great Cards

1. **Clear Input**: High-quality PDFs or structured notes yield better cards than messy screenshots.
2. **Specific Difficulty**: Choose "Detailed" for exam prep or "Simple" for quick overviews.
3. **Anki Integration**: Once generated, download the `.apkg` file and import it directly into Anki to start your Spaced Repetition (SRS) journey.
