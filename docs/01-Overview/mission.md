# 🎯 Mission & Purpose

MeshCards exists to solve the "Active Recall Bottleneck." While note-taking is common, transforming those notes into effective study materials is tedious and often ignored.

## 🏁 The Problem
1. **Flashcard Burnout**: Manually creating 50 cards for a single lecture takes hours.
2. **Passive Learning**: Most students just re-read their notes, which is the least effective study method.
3. **Generic Decks**: Public Anki decks often don't match exactly what you're learning in class or from your specific PDFs.

## 🚀 Our Solution
MeshCards acts as the **bridge** between your personal notes and long-term memory. 

> **The Mission**: To enable any student to move from a raw PDF to a high-quality, personalized Anki deck in under 60 seconds.

## 💎 Core Values
- **Personalized**: Cards should match your level—whether you're a beginner or an expert.
- **SRS-First**: We don't try to replace Anki; we empower it by producing standard `.apkg` files.
- **Aesthetic & Accessible**: A premium, "neobrutalist" interface that makes studying feel modern and alive.
- **Provider Agnostic**: Use our system keys or bring your own to ensure you're never blocked by a paywall.

## ✅ Success Criteria
MeshCards is successful when:
- Creating cards is no longer a chore, but an automatic part of your study workflow.
- You spend your time **reviewing** and **understanding**, not formatting and typing.
- Your Anki library is filled with cards that reflect your actual notes, not generic internet summaries.
